# Fellowship — goals

## seekers: a job seeker in web3 / the PL network finds roles that fit them
- **Customer:** someone looking for work at PL-network teams and the wider web3 world; results get posted to the PL network.
- **They ask:** "remote senior Rust roles", "devrel at a storage protocol", or they drop a resume and ask "what fits me?"
- **Failure looks like:** a resume match that returns off-field roles (a backend engineer shown sales jobs), stale or closed postings, or a whole category (e.g. PL network) missing.
- **Coverage:** every open role listed at os.pl.xyz/jobs, plus a broader set of web3 companies.
